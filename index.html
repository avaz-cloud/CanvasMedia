What we do

Everything a brand needs, under one roof.

Strategy first, always. Then design, build and content that carry it into the world, coherent from the logo on your door to the last frame of your campaign.

Brand Identity

Logo design and brand colours, along with the simple visual rules that hold them together, so every part of your brand feels like you.

Graphic Design

Layouts and artwork for print, packaging and campaigns, crafted with the detail obsessed over so you never have to. We design the prints; the actual printing is left to your printer of choice.

Website Design

Fast, elegant, easy-to-manage websites designed around your customers, and built to look after themselves long after launch.

Marketing Strategy

Positioning, planning and campaigns grounded in what your audience actually cares about, then measured, refined and repeated.

Social Media

We write, design and schedule your posts, then keep the page managed and the community engaged, so it stays active and looked after.

Photography

Product, place and people. Honest photography with an editorial eye, shot on-island.

How we work

Four steps. No mystery.

Good work comes from a clear process and honest conversation. Here's ours, from first coffee to launch day.

01

Discover listen first

We start with questions, not answers. Workshops, research and honest conversations about your business, your customers and where you want to be.

02

Design make it yours

Strategy becomes something you can see. Identity, layouts and prototypes, shared early and shaped together, with no big reveals and no surprises.

03

Develop build it properly

Websites, artwork and campaigns produced to a standard we'd put our own name to. Tested, accessible, fast.

04

Launch then keep going

We stay close after go-live, measuring what matters, refining what doesn't, and helping your brand grow as your business does.

About Canvas

A new Guernsey studio, here to help local businesses look and sound their best.

Canvas started with a simple idea. Any business on the island should be able to get thoughtful, well-made design and marketing without it feeling out of reach or overcomplicated.

These are early days, and honestly that is part of the appeal. Canvas is a young studio built on a real love of design and a lot of care for the work, here to help Guernsey businesses grow through branding, websites and marketing that feel considered and personal.

We take on a small number of projects at a time, so each one gets proper attention.
